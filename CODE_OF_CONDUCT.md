# Code of Conduct

Lamund adopts the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org),
version 2.1.

In short: be respectful and constructive. Harassment, discrimination, and
personal attacks are not tolerated in issues, pull requests, or any other
project space.

Report unacceptable behavior to **conduct@lamun.my.id**. Reports are handled
confidentially. Maintainers may remove, edit, or reject contributions and
may ban contributors for behavior they judge inappropriate.

The full text is available at
https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/.
